Sun Erniang 孙二娘

The innkeeper whose buns were famously not pork.

The great deeds 大事記

  1. ch. 27

    Buns That Were Not Pork

    十字坡黑店

    The innkeeper of Crossroads ridge whose buns were famously not pork — until the exile she drugged woke up inside his own sack.

  2. ch. 27

    Following in a Merchant's Disguise

    結義武松

    She lived to tell it, and followed him to the mountain in a merchant's disguise.

  3. ch. 118

    Fallen in the Qingxi Yard

    青溪殞命

    She died inside the Qingxi palace yard in the last week of the war.

結局 The ending

Killed at Qingxi, in the last of the battles the amnesty bought. She had kept the road inn where travellers woke up on the slab, and the book hands her a rank and an unremarkable death without once mentioning the inn again.
