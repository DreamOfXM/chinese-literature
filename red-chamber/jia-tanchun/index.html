Leaf 5 of the Red Chamber · 蕉下客 Guest Beneath the Plantains

Jia Tanchun 贾探春

The concubine-born daughter with a manager's hand on the tiller: she sees the house going under, patches what she can reach, and is carried off before the patch holds.

Origin
Daughter of Jia Zheng by the concubine Zhao; sister of Jia Huan, half-sister of Baoyu
Given
Claims Lady Wang as her only mother and refuses, to her own pain, the concubine's kin
Won
One season as deputy head of the household, with Li Wan and Baochai, while Xifeng was ill

The great deeds 大事記

  1. ch. 37

    The Card Founding the Poetry Club

    偶結海棠社

    Writes the card founding the poetry club, names herself Guest Beneath the Plantains for the trees at her window, and sets the sisters their first subject.

  2. ch. 55

    The Books Taken Up

    理家

    Given the books while Xifeng is laid up, her first act is to refuse extra mourning money for the concubine Zhao's dead brother, holding to the old precedent.

  3. ch. 56

    Letting the Garden Women Farm

    除弊

    Lets the garden women farm the bamboo groves and fields on a surplus quota, so the estate earns from what it used to waste — the one reform with a head on it.

  4. ch. 70

    The Phoenix Kite Let Go

    風箏

    Her phoenix kite goes up over the garden and the sisters slip their own strings to shed bad luck; the kite is lost in the distance and none of them likes it.

  5. ch. 74

    The Lantern at the Dawn Search

    抄檢

    Meets the dawn search of the daughters' rooms with a lantern, a slap for the old woman who groped at her clothes, and the warning that great houses kill themselves first.

結局 The ending

Her verse puts tears at the river bank at Qingming and a thousand li of east wind, one far dream. In the 120-chapter text she is betrothed to the son of a southern commander and goes away over the water, so that the kite let go in ch. 70 is the last thing she does inside the garden.
