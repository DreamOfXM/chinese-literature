唐僧旃檀功德佛 · 2

Leaf 2 of the pilgrimage · 唐三藏 Tripitaka of the Tang

Tang Sanzang 唐僧

The only pilgrim with no powers, who nevertheless is the one who decides when the story may end.

Origin
the Golden Cicada, second of the Buddha's disciples
Borne
ten lives of toil for yawning during a lecture
Wears
the 錦襴袈裟 and the fillet he cannot remove

The great deeds 大事記

  1. ch. 9

    Set Adrift on a Plank

    陳光蕊赴任

    An infant set adrift on a plank by his mother, saved by a monk at Jinshan and raised as the river-boy Jiangliu.

  2. ch. 12

    The Oath and the Undated Road

    玄奘立誓

    Named Tripitaka, adopted as the emperor's brother, and he leaves without saying how long it will take.

  3. ch. 14

    Naming the Monkey

    收猴

    He gives Wukong a name and a collar in the same breath, and does not understand either of them for forty years.

  4. ch. 27

    Writes His Own Expulsion Letter

    貶書逐徒

    He writes the expulsion letter with his own hand, and is kidnapped eleven days later.

  5. ch. 47

    Weeping for Chen Village

    替人間災

    He weeps at Chen Village over the boy and girl due to the river god, and his disciples take his place instead.

  6. ch. 78

    Refusing the Heart Elixir

    童心之藥

    He refuses an elixir made of 1,111 children's hearts so flatly that even Wukong is surprised.

  7. ch. 99

    Climbing Vulture Peak

    登鷲嶺

    He walks the last approach on his own two feet, and is the one who must be told the scriptures are wrong.

結局 The ending

He completes the journey, drowns once on the final river, and is made the Candana Merit Buddha. His last recorded act in the world is to fall off a horse and be rescued by his own disciples, which the novel does not treat as a joke.
