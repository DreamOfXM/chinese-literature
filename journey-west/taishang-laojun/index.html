Leaf 8 of the pilgrimage · 道祖 The Patriarch of the Way

Taishang Laojun 太上老君

In Journey to the West 西遊記 (c. 1592) · a heaven-sent power who ends the fight · nicknamed 道祖 “The Patriarch of the Way”

The Taoist patriarch whose scrapes are the journey's backbone: the monkey was cooked in his furnace, and everything that ever disarmed Wukong came out of his house.

Seat
the Tushita Palace above the Heaven of Resentment
Keeps
the diamond snare, the eight-trigram furnace, two boy servants
Servants lost
two to Buddha's test, one to his own ox-boy's nap

The great deeds 大事記

  1. ch. 6

    Casting Down the Diamond Ring

    金剛琢

    He drops the diamond snare from heaven and it catches Wukong on the head, which is how the capture ends up working.

  2. ch. 7

    The Eight-Trigram Furnace

    煉丹失猴

    He put the monkey in the eight-trigram furnace to be destroyed; the furnace is why Wukong can see through every disguise in the book.

  3. ch. 35

    Reclaiming the Two Horned Boys

    收二童

    He comes down for the gold and silver horns, shakes his sleeve, and the two kings become two boys again.

  4. ch. 39

    The Nine-Turn Soul-Return Pill

    九轉還魂丹

    He supplies the pill that restarts a king dead three years in a well, and is not thanked for it.

  5. ch. 52

    The Plantain Fan for the Ox

    失青牛

    He discovers his ox has gone only when Wukong's staff turns up missing too, and comes down with a plantain fan to call it back.

結局 The ending

He is the only power in the book who loses things repeatedly and remains indispensable: the furnace, the snare, the ox, the boys. His last appearance is a pill handed over without a sermon attached.
